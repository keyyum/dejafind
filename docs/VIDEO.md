# Demo video, 60 seconds

Owner: story pane from 07:15, with the founder on camera for the phone shot. Deliver `video/dejafind-demo.mp4`, 1920×1080, 30 fps, under 60 s, captions burned in, no music louder than the voice.

## Record

- Screen: `screencapture -v` or QuickTime, Library open full screen in Chrome at 1920×1080, 125% zoom so text reads on phones. Record the real app, never the mockup.
- Phone shot: founder's phone filming the laptop and his hand taking the screenshot, 10 seconds, landscape.
- Record each beat twice. Keep the best take.

HyperFrames for the title cards and captions over the screen recording, or iMovie if faster. Captions: Figtree, white on a dark band, 48px. Export H.264. Check it plays on a phone with sound off, since most judges will watch it that way.
