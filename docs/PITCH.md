# Pitch, 5 minutes plus 3 minutes Q&A

Owner: story pane builds `pitch/index.html` as six full-screen slides in the brand, keyboard-advanced, offline. The founder presents. The demo is live in the real app, the slides only frame it.

## Slides

1. **Name.** Mark, DéjàFind, "Find what you already saw." Team names.
2. **The pain.** One photo of a camera roll full of slides. "You photograph everything. You never open it again. And the quiz date was in a group chat three weeks ago."
3. **What it does.** Three words on one line: Watches. Understands. Finds. Under it: "No upload. No internet."
4. **Live demo.** Blank slide with the word DEMO. Switch to the app. Follow the 60-second script in `docs/PLAN.md`, slower, with narration.
5. **Why local.** Three lines: A website can't watch your folders. Under half of Filipino homes have internet. Your notes never leave your laptop. Small print: qwen2.5-vl 3B, qwen3 4B, nomic-embed, measured N s per document on an M4 16 GB.
6. **What's different, and what's next.** Left: Pixel Screenshots (Pixel 9 only, 15 a day), NotebookLM (cloud, manual), Lens retired. "The only one we found that does all of this offline." Right: phone app next, same models. Close: "Built by students, for students."

## Timing

0:00 hook and pain, 0:50 what it does, 1:10 demo until 3:40, 3:40 why local, 4:10 different and next, 4:40 close.

## Q&A answers, one line each

- Spotlight does this: keyword only, no subjects, no deadlines, no answers, no quiz.
- Pixel does this: one phone family, 15 images a day, manual filing.
- Hallucination: answers are restricted to your documents and cite them, or it says not in your documents.
- Handwriting: printed slides and screenshots read well; handwriting is next and we won't quote accuracy we haven't measured.
- Phone: same 2 to 4B models already run on flagship Androids; the laptop was tonight's scope.
- Receipts and non-school files: indexed and searchable under Other; we built for class.
- Accuracy numbers: measured on N of our own documents on this machine; we have no public benchmark and won't invent one.
